Privacy Policy

Cat Troubles (猫的烦恼)
iOS app, bundle ID com.numar.catTroubles
Effective 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the iOS version of Cat Troubles does with information. It covers the app distributed on the Apple App Store. It does not describe any other edition of the game.

Who we are

The developer of Cat Troubles (“we”) is the seller of this app on the App Store. We do not create an account for you, and we do not run our own server for this iOS app. Progress is stored in your personal iCloud, and scores are handled by Game Center. Both services are operated by Apple.

Information used to provide the game

If you use an Apple Account, the app saves a game record in your iCloud Key-Value Store. That record can include:

  • Campaign progress, such as levels passed, stars, and opened cells
  • Free-play progress
  • The board and theme you have equipped
  • Whether tutorial prompts have been shown
  • Whether a sidebar reward has been claimed

The same record is also kept on the device. We cannot read your iCloud store from a server of our own.

When you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your campaign score and your free-play score to Apple’s leaderboards. It reads your Game Center display name, and, if you have one, your Game Center profile photo, so it can show them in Settings and on your own leaderboard row. The photo is saved only as a temporary file on the device. We do not upload your name or photo to a server of our own.

If you are not signed in to Game Center, the app does not receive your Apple Account name or photo. Your row uses the game’s own default portrait and the label “Me”.

Information that stays on the device

Music, sound effects, and vibration settings stay on the device. They are not written to iCloud and are not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

This version of the app does not read your contacts, location, photo library, camera, or microphone. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. It does not include advertising, and it does not include purchases. It does not sell personal information.

Why this information is used

The iCloud record is used to keep your progress, equipped board, and theme when you reinstall the app or use another device with the same Apple Account. Game Center scores, your display name, and your profile photo are used to show leaderboards and your profile in the game.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the performance of the game you chose to play for these features. You do not have to accept a separate privacy consent form before you can play. Apple processes iCloud and Game Center data under Apple’s own privacy policy.

How long it is kept

The iCloud record remains in your Apple Account until you delete that data, or until a later session overwrites it. Leaderboard scores remain with Game Center under Apple’s rules. The temporary profile photo can be removed by iOS with other temporary files. Settings that stay on the device remain until you change them or delete the app.

Your choices

  • You can turn Game Center off for this app in iOS Settings. Scores, your display name, and your profile photo will then not be used.
  • You can manage or delete the app’s iCloud data from iOS Settings.
  • You can delete the app to remove the copy stored on that device, including music, sound, and vibration settings.

Your rights

If the EU or UK GDPR applies to you, you can ask to access, correct, or delete personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive a portable copy where that right applies. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not keep a separate copy of your save or your Game Center profile, the practical way to delete them is through iCloud and Game Center. Email us if you want help doing that.

We are not established in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. If we are required to appoint a representative there, that representative’s contact details will be added to this page.

Residents of US states with a consumer privacy law can email us to ask what this app collects and to ask us to delete it, where that law applies. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

Cat Troubles is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this app starts using information in a new way, including advertising or purchases, we will update this page and change the effective date before that version is released.
